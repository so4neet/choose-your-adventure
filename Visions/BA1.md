# Glasses

## You find glasses in your pocket.

They're thin, round framed glasses, but the lenses are completely black. Upon trying them on, you notice that not only can you see through them, but you can now see writing on the wall. It reads:

> Don't trust the ticking, they'll find you. Look under the desk.

You also see an X on the other wall, but there's nothing else near it.
